How forms meet in battle

Adult forms counter each other

Power beats Guard beats Speed beats Wits beats Power

Each adult move answers one other form: Power Slam breaks Shell Up, Shell Up hurts Flurry's many hits, Flurry makes a slow thinker flinch, and Mind Trick turns a strong pet's Power against it. Opposite forms are about even.

Elements

Tide beats Ember beats Bloom beats Tide

An element move does ×1.2 against the element it beats and ×0.83 the other way. Astral has no strength or weakness.

Growing stronger

Health is 74 + 15 per life stage up to adult + 0.2 × Guard. Stats are capped at 30 for a child, 60 for a teen and 100 from adult on. A child knows 2 moves, a teen 3, an adult 4.
